Trevarefabrikken & Lofoten Jazzklubb will be joining forces and welcoming you all to a jazzy weekend at the factory on September 11th & 12th!
In September we'll be host to a friendly bunch of talented musicians. They will transform the factory for two days, continuing our fall concept from last year - Live jazz for a full weekend at Trevarefabrikken!

There will be plenty of live jazz, both mellow and groovy, familiar and up&coming, as well as Jazz Lounge- and club sets from our good friend Espen Horne and his concept Jassbevegelsen. Jazz of all sorts both Friday and Saturday!

### MARIUS NESET & ANTON EGER DUO

When Marius Neset and Anton Eger take the stage together, pure musical power is unleashed. The duo is built on a long-standing and close collaboration — not least through Neset’s solo career, where Eger is a permanent member, the award-winning band Phronesis, and JazzKamikaze — where they developed a telepathic interplay marked by virtuosity, energy, and uncompromising creativity.

See them at Trevarejazz on Friday 11th at 22.00.
See Marius & Anton perform together here: https://bit.ly/4g12E4r

### ALLEXA NAVA

Allexa Nava is a saxophonist, flautist and composer, originally from Peru and currently residing in London. She has already graced the stages of some of the most significant festivals in the UK, including Glastonbury We Out Here, Wilderness, and Brainchild. She has also performed at some of the most prestigious venues in London and Paris including Ronnie Scotts, Barbican & Duc de Lombards. Catch her first ever show in Norway at Trevarejazz 2026!

See Allexa Nava on Saturday 12th at 20.30
Check out her live performance here: https://bit.ly/allexanava

### MODAL TRAFIKK

Modal Trafikk creates the kind of music that feels increasingly rare: fully alive, deeply rhythmic and completely built around collective movement. What started as one of Norway’s strongest afrobeat bands has evolved into something far broader and more unpredictable — a powerful fusion of African rhythms, Latin American grooves, psychedelic textures and electronic club energy.

Catch Modal Trafikk at Trevarejazz Saturday 12th at 22.30
Check out their music here: https://bit.ly/Modaltrafikk

### ISBIT

isbit blends jazz, funk, and pop into a fresh, atmospheric soundscape that feels both playful and organic. With strong melodies, a solid groove at its core, and a collective approach, they create music that feels both youthful and genre-defying. The band released their first single in the summer of 2025 and already plans to continue building their universe with new releases and concerts. Performing at the biggest showcase festival in Norway, By:Larm, the day before they will also showcase themselves for the first time in the north at Friday during Trevarejazz.

Catch Isbit on Friday 11th at 20.30
Check out their music here: https://bit.ly/isbit-trevarejazz
